How does sheet sync work?
Share the Google Sheet with “Anyone with the link can view”, then paste its URL above and hit Sync sheet. Pulse reads the Dashboard-data tab specifically and looks for these columns (any order, any casing):
DateAssetSideMarginFeesEntry PriceExit PriceNet Profit
Side is Long, Short or Transfer. Net Profit (not Gross) is used as the realised P&L — Fees is parsed and shown for reference but not subtracted again, since it's already reflected there. If your Margin column actually holds a leverage multiplier like 40x rather than a ₹ amount, Pulse detects that automatically and uses it as leverage instead — falling back to |P&L| as a rough size proxy for the asset bars, since there's no real capital figure to total in that case. Entry Price / Exit Price show up in the Activity tab, currency symbols and commas included. Optional Status is picked up too if present; rows with Status = Open are left out of realised totals until closed. Everything on this page — totals, the daily grid, the chart and the asset list — is recalculated live from those rows.
